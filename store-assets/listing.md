# Front Path — CrazyGames listing

**Title:** Front Path

**Short description:** Tap arrows to slide them off the board — but only if their path is clear. Plan the order and clear every arrow!

**Description:**
Front Path is a calm but tricky logic puzzle. Every arrow on the board slides straight in the direction it points when you tap it. If another arrow is in the way, it's blocked. Figure out the right order to clear the whole board. 150 hand-checked, always-solvable levels across Easy, Medium and Hard (by how full the board is), with tall phone-friendly boards that grow as you progress. Play relaxed in Normal mode with a stopwatch, or race the clock in Timed mode — and watch an ad for +15 seconds when time runs out. Your progress and best times are saved.

**Controls:**
- Mouse: click an arrow to slide it.
- Touch: tap an arrow to slide it.
- You can tap several arrows in a row — they slide at the same time.

**Tags:** Puzzle, Logic, Arrow, Brain, Casual, Relaxing, Mobile, Minimalist, Timed, One-Button
